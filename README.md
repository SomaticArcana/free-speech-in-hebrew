# Free Speech in Hebrew

An unofficial study companion to the speak-first Hebrew method taught by Alana Shuster, PhD,
author of the course book *Free Speech in Hebrew, Level 1* (Cognella, 2018). The method was created by Zev bar-Lev.

**Open the app:** https://somaticarcana.github.io/free-speech-in-hebrew/

It runs in any phone or computer browser, needs no account, and can be added to the home screen.
After the first visit it works without an internet connection. Progress is saved on the device.

## What this is, and is not
